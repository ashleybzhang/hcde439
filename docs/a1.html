1. Circuit Schematic & Physical Breadboard

Circuit Layout Diagram: This physical breadboard layout shows an Arduino Uno controlling three independent light channels. The Red LED is driven by Digital Pin 8, the Yellow LED by Pin 10, and the Green LED by Pin 9. Jumper wires carry signals from these pins to the positive anode legs of the LEDs. A single black wire returns current from the common negative rail back into the Arduino GND pin.
Circuit Schematic: This formal circuit schematic shows three parallel LED paths connected directly to the Arduino's I/O pins. The resistors are placed in series directly before the LEDs to limit current flow and protect components from burning out. Based on Ohm's law (R = (V_source - V_f) / I), 220Ω resistors are utilized for the Red and Yellow lines to target a safe operating current of ~15mA from the 5V logic supply, while a 120Ω or 150Ω resistor is specified for the Green channel to offset its higher forward operating voltage.
