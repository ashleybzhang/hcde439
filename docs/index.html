Ashley Zhang's HCDE 439 Physical Computing Page

Hi, I'm Ashley, a 4th year HCDE major at the University of Washington. I'm excited to learn new tools and concepts in physical computing and to explore how they can shape the way people interact with products.

A1: Blink!

Making an LED blink on a timer using an Arduino.

A2: Fade!

A night-light prototype where one LED breathes with PWM and a button lights two more.

A3: Dusk-o-meter

A night light that senses how dark the room is with a photoresistor and fades three LEDs up as it gets darker.

A4: Smart Fan

A small fan that turns on with a touch and speeds up as the room gets brighter, using a touch pad, a photoresistor and a servo.
